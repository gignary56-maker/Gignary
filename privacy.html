Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Gignary collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and password (stored securely, never in plain text) when you sign up.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from your Google account. We never receive your Google password.
  • Profile information: avatar, bio, skills, language, and phone number, if you choose to add them.
  • Activity data: gigs you post, orders you place, messages you send through the Platform, and reports you submit.
  • Uploaded files: photos and videos on your gigs and profile, and payment receipt screenshots you upload for orders.
  • Technical data: basic device and browser information used to keep the Platform secure and working properly.

2. How we use this information

  • To create and manage your account and show your profile to other users where relevant.
  • To let Buyers and Sellers find each other, message, and track orders.
  • To review reports submitted through the Report feature and take appropriate action.
  • To send you account-related notifications (such as order updates or report status).

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Some information — like your display name, bio, and gigs — is visible to other users because that's how the marketplace works. We may share information if required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

4. Data storage and third-party services

Account and platform data is stored with our database provider (Supabase) using industry-standard security practices, including encrypted connections. Files you upload, such as gig media, profile pictures and payment receipts, are stored in Supabase Storage. If you sign in with Google, Google processes that sign-in under its own privacy policy.

5. Your choices

  • You can update or remove your profile information at any time from Settings.
  • You can request account deletion by contacting us at hello@gignary.com.

6. Cookies and local storage

Gignary uses your browser's local storage to keep you logged in between visits. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll post the updated version here with a new "last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to hello@gignary.com.
