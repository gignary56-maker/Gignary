How it works

Two sides, one clear process

Whether you're hiring or selling, here is exactly what happens.

If you're a buyer

  1. Browse gigs or post a request with your budget.
  2. Message the seller and confirm the details.
  3. Place your order and pay using the method shown.
  4. Our team verifies your payment, then the seller starts.
  5. Track your order and chat with the seller until delivery.

If you're a seller

  1. Create your profile and post a gig with a photo or video.
  2. Answer buyer messages and agree on the scope.
  3. Receive orders once the buyer's payment is verified.
  4. Deliver the work and keep the buyer updated in chat.
