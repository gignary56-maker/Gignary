Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Contact support.

What is Gignary?

Gignary is a marketplace where buyers hire freelancers (sellers) for design, writing, marketing, development and more. You can browse gigs, message sellers, place orders and track them from start to finish.

Is it free to sign up?

Yes. Creating an account is free, and you can sign up with your email or with Google.

How do I place an order?

Open a gig, message the seller if you want to confirm details, then place your order. You will get a tracking ID and see the payment steps right away.

How does payment work?

You pay Gignary using the payment methods shown when you place your order, then upload a screenshot of your receipt. Our team verifies your payment first. Once it is confirmed, your order is sent to the seller. Please do not pay a seller directly outside Gignary.

When does the seller start working?

After our team confirms your payment. Your order is then sent to the seller and you continue the conversation in the Conversation tab.

How do I track my order?

Go to Profile → Order status. Each order shows its tracking ID and current stage.

What if the seller does not deliver, or I have a problem?

Use “Report a problem with this order” on your Order status page. Our team reviews the case and, if the seller is at fault, can refund your payment. The full steps are in the Refund Policy.

How do I post a gig as a seller?

Log in, open your dashboard and choose to add a gig. Add a title, category, price, description and a photo or video. See the Buyer and Seller guides for the full walkthrough.

How do I report a profile or gig?

Use the Report option in the menu on a gig or profile. Reports go straight to our team, who review them and take action where needed.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the login page, type your email and click “Forgot password?”. We will email you a link to set a new password.

How do I delete my account?

Email hello@gignary.com from the address on your account and we will help you.
