Buyer Guide

How to hire on Gignary, step by step

1. Create your account

Sign up with your email or Google. It takes under a minute.

2. Browse gigs or post a request

Explore gigs by category (Design, Writing, Marketing, Development, Video Editor, UI/UX Designer, Banner), or post what you need.

3. Message the seller

Confirm exactly what is included, the timeline and the price in Conversation before you order. This avoids most disputes.

4. Place your order and pay

Place your order, pay the amount shown to the JazzCash or Easypaisa account at checkout, and upload your receipt. Pay only to the details shown on Gignary.

5. We verify your payment

Our team verifies your payment first. Once confirmed, your order is sent to the seller in Conversation.

6. Track and receive your order

Follow your order under Profile → Order status: Received, Reviewed, In progress, Delivered.

7. If something goes wrong

Use "Report a problem with this order". Our team reviews the case and refunds you if the seller is at fault. See the Refund Policy.
