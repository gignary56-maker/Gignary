FAQ & Support

Answers to common questions

How do I pay for an order?

After you place an order, pay the amount shown to the JazzCash or Easypaisa account listed at checkout, choose which method you used, and upload a screenshot of your receipt.

What happens after I pay?

Our team verifies your payment first. Once confirmed, your order is sent to the seller in Conversation, and you can follow its status under Profile → Order status.

Is my payment protected?

Payments are verified by our team. If a seller does not deliver or the work is substantially different from what was agreed, report it and we will review the case. If the seller is at fault, you are refunded. See the Refund Policy. Never pay a seller outside Gignary.

What if there is a problem with my order?

Use "Report a problem with this order" on your Order status page, or Report in the profile menu. Our team reviews the case, contacts both sides, and updates you on the outcome.

How do I post a gig as a seller?

Log in, open your dashboard, and add a gig with a title, category, starting price, and a photo or video of your work.

How do I track my order?

Go to Profile → Order status to see your order ID and current stage: Received, Reviewed, In progress, or Delivered.

How do I contact support?

Email hello@gignary.com. Include your order ID if your question is about an order.
