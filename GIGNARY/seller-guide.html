Seller Guide

How to sell your work on Gignary

1. Create your account and profile

Sign up, then add your bio, skills and language so buyers know who you are.

2. Post a gig

Add a title, description, category, starting price and a photo or video showing your work.

3. Answer buyer messages

Buyers may message you in Conversation before ordering. Reply clearly and confirm scope, timeline and price.

4. Receive verified orders

Orders reach you in Conversation only after our team has verified the buyer's payment.

5. Deliver as agreed

Complete the work as agreed and keep communication in Conversation, so there is a record if a dispute comes up.

6. Reports and disputes

Buyers can report problems with an order. Our team reviews the conversation and may contact you for your side. Accounts that break the Terms can be suspended.
