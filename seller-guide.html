Seller Guide

How to sell your work on Gignary

1. Get your seller account

Seller accounts are created by the Gignary team. We send you a login email and password. Log in from the Log in page and you land on your seller dashboard. Please change your password first (Profile → Change password).

2. Complete your profile

Add your bio, skills, language and a profile photo so buyers know who you are. Your seller name is set by Gignary.

3. Post your gigs

Go to My gigs → New gig. Add a title, category, starting price, description and a photo or video showing your work.

4. Receive verified orders

Orders appear in your Orders tab only after our team has verified the buyer's payment, and you get a notification. Accept the order to start.

5. Chat and deliver

Talk to the buyer in Messages. When the work is ready, send the final file as a PDF in the chat, then mark the order as Delivered. The buyer sees each step (Received, Reviewed, In progress, Delivered).

6. Keep contact details private

All communication stays inside Gignary. Phone numbers, email addresses and social media links cannot be shared in messages, gigs or your profile, and they are blocked automatically. Never ask a buyer to pay outside Gignary. Accounts that try to move buyers off the platform can be suspended.

7. Reports and disputes

Buyers can report problems with an order. Our team reviews the conversation and may contact you for your side. Accounts that break the Terms can be suspended.
