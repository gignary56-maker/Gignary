Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Refund Policy explains how refund requests are generally handled for orders placed through Gignary. Because each gig and order is unique, refunds are considered on a case-by-case basis.

1. Before you order

We encourage Buyers to message the Seller and confirm exactly what's included, the timeline, and the price before placing an order — this avoids most disputes.

2. When a refund may apply

  • The Seller does not deliver the agreed work within a reasonable time and stops responding.
  • The delivered work is substantially different from what was agreed and the Seller is unwilling to revise it.
  • The order was placed in error (e.g. duplicate order) and no work has started yet.

3. When a refund typically does not apply

  • Work was delivered as agreed but the Buyer simply changed their mind.
  • The Buyer did not provide information or feedback needed for the Seller to complete the work.
  • Minor differences in style or interpretation that fall within what was reasonably agreed.

4. How to request a refund or raise a dispute

Use the "Report a problem with this order" option from your Order status page, or the Report option from the profile menu, describing what happened. Our team reviews each case and will reach out with next steps.

5. Timeline

We aim to review refund and dispute reports promptly. Response times may vary depending on the complexity of the order.

6. Contact

For anything not covered here, reach out to hello@gignary.com.
